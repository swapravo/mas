# Abandoned-cart email

**Agents:** Backend, Database, Security, QA

Send a reminder email 2 hours after a user leaves items in their cart without buying. Carts are saved for logged-in users and for anonymous browsers (by cookie). Users can buy on the website or in the app. Emails go through a third-party provider that bills per email. Our customers are spread across India, the UK and the US. Some users have unsubscribed from marketing emails, and there is a separate setting for order emails.
